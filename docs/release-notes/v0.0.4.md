Cross-section drawings you can hand in, undo for everything, a view cube, terrain from point clouds, and a complete railway sample built after Banedanmark's norms.

### Cross sections and drawings
- **Drawing presets and a sheet view (#51).**
  - *Rail cross section A3 1:100*, *Overview* and your own presets keep all drawing, annotation and label settings.
  - Presets are saved in the browser and in the project, and can be exported and imported as `.vispecta-preset.json`.
  - 📄 in the section window shows the section exactly as the sheet it will be exported as.
- **Layout, styles and hatching (#50).**
  - Hatched cut faces in PDF, SVG and DXF.
  - Line weights, the band under or over the section, and text size.
  - A dimension chain, and labels spread out with kinked leaders.
  - DXF layers per annotation type.
- **Label content and number format (#49):**
  - offset, height and distance;
  - zero points (axis, edge, track centre, top of rail);
  - decimals, m or mm, prefixes such as `K=`, and slopes in %, ‰ or 1:n.
- **Choose the annotated points (#48).** In the section window, click to remove or add a point. The choice is kept per station and in the project.
- **Exact alignments (#79).** Cross sections in curves were up to 11 cm off the axis. The horizontal alignment is now computed from its design parameters (lines, arcs, clothoids).
- **Width of cross sections (#66):**
  - the section is limited to left | right of the alignment;
  - ✂ turns off the 3D cut of a station section and keeps the section (#39);
  - zoom and pan in the section window (#65).
- **Section window in the app:** a new cross section opens next to the 3D view. Sections are faster, because cut faces are computed once, with an outline along the cut faces in 3D (#68).

### Working in the model
- **Undo and redo (#67):** Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y and ↶ ↷ cover hiding, colours, sections, measurements, drawn lines and the corridor.
- **View cube (#84)** in the top right corner:
  - click a face, edge or corner to look from there;
  - drag to orbit, turn the view 90°, or go home;
  - a compass ring shows north.
- **Several alignments in a model (#89):** switch each on or off and give it an alias. Station labels of parallel tracks no longer collide.
- **Drawn lines in the model browser (#74):** show/hide, rename, zoom to, edit points and remove.
- **Dialogs close with ✕** in the top right corner (or Esc).
- **Offset lines and corridors (#26)** with separate fields for left and right.
- **Stationing formats (#29).**
- **A warning when models lie far apart (#28).**

### Terrain and point clouds
- **Point clouds (#27):** LAS, LAZ, XYZ/CSV/TXT/PTS and PLY next to the IFC models.
  - They are placed in IFC or map coordinates.
  - They are coloured by RGB, class, intensity or height.
  - They show in sections, drawings, profiles and measurements.
- **Terrain (TIN) from point clouds and LandXML (#45):**
  - ground points are thinned and triangulated in a worker;
  - the result is written as an IFC model (IfcGeographicElement TERRAIN, with the models' map conversion);
  - it shows as a terrain line in sections, drawings and profiles, and can be downloaded as IFC.
- **Terrain opens in a calm green (#90).** Height colours (blue – red or green – brown – white) are a choice on the terrain's card.

### Samples
- **Double-track railway with a bridge (#87, #88).** A fictitious 1 km line for 180 km/h after Banedanmark norms (BN1-6-7, BN1-11, BN1-13, BN1-59, BN1-160, KI-Sicat). One IFC 4.3 file per discipline:
  - alignment;
  - track: 60E1 rails, S99 sleepers, welds and km marks;
  - earthworks with transition wedges and topsoil;
  - drainage;
  - a 20 m bridge;
  - overhead contact line;
  - cables;
  - terrain and landscape.

  A survey point cloud in UTM coordinates comes with it.
- **Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel**, a synthetic 31 km corridor from buildingSMART's community samples.
