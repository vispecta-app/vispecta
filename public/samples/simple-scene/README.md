# Sample models: IFC 4.3 Simple Scene

The models in this folder are the "Simple-Scene" sample of IFC 4.3.2.0 (IFC 4.3 ADD2) from
buildingSMART International's sample test files: https://github.com/buildingSMART/Sample-Test-Files

(C) buildingSMART International Ltd. Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The ten scene files are unchanged. buildingSMART International does not endorse Vispecta. Vispecta's "Try sample models" button on the start screen loads them.

## Infra-Alignment.ifc (added by Vispecta)

`Infra-Alignment.ifc` is not part of buildingSMART's scene. Vispecta made it so that the sample can
show stationing, cross sections and longitudinal profiles: an IFC 4.3 `IfcAlignment` named "Track"
along the centre line of the rails in `Infra-Rail.ifc` – straight, 40 m, at top of rail, stations
0+000 to 0+040 – with the same units and map conversion as the scene. It is generated by
`scripts/make-sample-alignment.py` and shared under CC BY 4.0 like the scene.
