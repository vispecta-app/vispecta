# Fictitious double-track railway, 180 km/h (Banedanmark norms)

A 1 km double-track ballasted railway (12+000 – 13+000) with a 20 m bridge over a stream, drawn up by
Vispecta to show what an infrastructure model can hold. **Everything is fictitious**: the alignment,
the terrain, the survey and the stream are generated, and values that the norms leave open are
assumed and marked as such in the property sets. The model follows the Banedanmark norms below as
far as a simple model can; it is not a design and not checked by Banedanmark. Element names and
property sets are in Danish, as in Danish rail projects.

Generated by [`scripts/samples/make_double_track_railway.py`](../../../scripts/samples/make_double_track_railway.py)
(IfcOpenShell). Georeferenced with IfcMapConversion to ETRS89 / UTM zone 32N (E 680 000, N 6 150 000),
heights DVR90.

## Files – one per discipline

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `01-Alignment.ifc` | Two IfcAlignment (track 1 and 2): line, clothoid 150 m, arc R = 2000 m, clothoid, line; vertical curves R = 20 000 m; cant 110 mm; stationing from 12+000 |
| `02-Track.ifc` | Ballast, 60E1 rails (1:40), 3 200 S99 sleepers (one mapped type), aluminothermic welds every 120 m, km marks |
| `03-Earthworks.ifc` | Sub-ballast (stable gravel and sub-base, 2 × 0.20 m), transition wedges at the bridge, embankment, topsoil 0.20 m on the slopes |
| `04-Drainage.ifc` | Ditches, wells with sand traps, outlet pipes, a lined outlet into the stream, filter and drain pipes behind the abutments |
| `05-Bridge.ifc` | A 20 m slab bridge: deck, edge beams, waterproofing, protection concrete, abutments, wing walls, bearings, railings with ballast screens, stairs with handrails |
| `06-Overhead-line.ifc` | Sicat SX: foundations, masts, K-ophæng (cantilevers with insulators), contact wire, messenger, droppers |
| `07-Cables.ifc` | Cable troughs and cable ducts in the edge beams of the bridge |
| `08-Terrain.ifc` | The terrain surface with the ditches cut in (IfcGeographicElement TERRAIN) |
| `09-Landscape.ifc` | Trees and the stream |
| `10-Survey.laz` | A survey of the existing terrain before construction: 182 000 points in UTM coordinates, classes ground, vegetation, water and noise, RGB and intensity |

## Norms

| Norm | Used for |
|---|---|
| BN1-6-7 (01.04.2023) | Cross section 160 < V ≤ 200 km/h: Bsk 0.55 m, ballast slope 1:1.5, Bt ≥ 0.35 m, Ut 0.40 m as two layers of 0.20 m (note 9), Pb 3.80 m, 40 ‰; transition zones at structures Loz = 17 m (15.1, figure 12.1-1); mast foundations ≥ 2.70 m from the track centre (note 1) |
| BN1-11-1 (01.10.2006) | Ditches in fill (Bilag 1.1): bottom 0.35 m, depth ≥ 0.50 m, slopes 1:1.5, fall ≥ 2 ‰, wells ≥ ø300 with sand traps, concrete slab lining where a ditch meets a larger inflow (12.1) |
| BN1-13-4 (23.07.2024) | Cable troughs over ground: outside the planum width, ≥ 3.25 m from the nearest rail (V > 120 km/h), ≥ 2.50 m from the track centre, ≤ 0.20 m above the ground |
| BN1-59-5 (20.11.2024) | Bridge: free width 4.00 + fs + 4.00 m, service passage 0.80 m, edge upstand 300 + 200 mm, ≥ 2.20 m from CL track to fixed objects, SO to deck ≥ 0.75 m, falls 25 / 10 / 110 ‰, stairs, ≥ 100 mm ballast over the deck |
| BN1-160-1 (01.09.2008) | Km marks every 200 m, 3.0 m from the nearest track centre, 2 m above SO, sides by hectometer digit, on the railing on the bridge |
| BN1-214-3 (04.02.2026) | Sicat SX components; contact wire height, system height and spans are assumed |
| Sporteknik, GAB Spor bilag 0-1 | S99 sleeper 2500 × 300 × 206 mm, rail inclination 1:40 |
